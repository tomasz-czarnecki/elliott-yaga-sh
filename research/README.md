# Research

Background documents for the Elliott Wave analyzer. The rules for working in this repo are in `../CLAUDE.md`.

- [`probabilistic-elliott-wave-engine-plan.md`](probabilistic-elliott-wave-engine-plan.md): the master plan. Architecture, data structures, Phases 0–5, evaluation, published evidence on Elliott and Fibonacci claims, and where LLMs fit.
- [`btc-15m-history-data-sources.md`](btc-15m-history-data-sources.md): where the BTC data comes from, sources ranked, the limits of each.
- [`cycles-r1.md`](cycles-r1.md): 4-year, weekly and daily cycles: how they are marked, how the marks compare with random paths, the current state, how to read the chart overlay.
- [`counts-r1.md`](counts-r1.md): the top-down count board. 1D or 12H counts stepped down through 4H, 1H and 15m, with crossroads and Y's: how it works, every decision, the current tree, a replay at past dates, the check against random paths, the honest limits.
- [`counts-r2.md`](counts-r2.md): round 2 of the count board, Fibonacci time as its own axis: the time inventory, price, time and structure sub-scores, time windows and crosshairs, r1 against r2, a replay and the random-path check.
- [`counts-r3.md`](counts-r3.md): round 3, rules kill and time ranks: his premise as the root, his gold counts as a regression test, the exact cause of r2 losing his 5, r2 against r3, a replay and the random-path check.
- [`counts-r4.md`](counts-r4.md): round 4, what is inside a wave is a rule: the inside-rule table with its sources, the search down the frames, the time minimum as a guideline, r3 against r4, what died at each frame, his gold counts wave by wave, export timing and the honest limits.
